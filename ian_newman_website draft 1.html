Professional Summary

Copper at the Core of the Energy Transition

Copper is not merely a commodity; it is the indispensable foundation of global decarbonization. Every electric vehicle requires three to four times the copper of a conventional automobile. Every wind turbine, solar installation, and grid-scale battery storage system depends on vast quantities of high-quality copper. The International Energy Agency projects that demand will nearly double by 2040, making the discovery and development of new copper resources one of the most urgent industrial challenges of our era.

I am an exploration geologist with U.S. and Canada dual citizenship and seven years of expertise concentrated on copper systems, precisely the deposit style responsible for the majority of the world's copper supply. Holding an M.Sc. in Geology from Laurentian University and a B.Sc. from San Jose State, I bring both rigorous academic grounding and proven real-world experience across some of North America's most significant copper exploration campaigns.

At Freeport-McMoRan, one of the world's largest producers of copper, I advanced from Geologist I to Geologist II, contributing to exploration programs at Lone Star, Chino, and Morenci. My command of Leapfrog Geo, MinePlan 3D, and acQuire GIM Suite enables me to translate raw drill data into actionable 3D geological models that drive capital-efficient drill planning decisions. I am a top-tier candidate for any organization seeking to advance copper projects in response to the extraordinary demand signals driving the global energy transition.
